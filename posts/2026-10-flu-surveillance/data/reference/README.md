# Reference values (hand-transcribed)

`cdc_ili_baselines_2025_26.csv`: CDC's 2025-26 ILINet baselines, copied from
[U.S. Influenza Surveillance: Purpose and Methods](https://www.cdc.gov/fluview/overview/index.html)
(page dated Dec 2, 2025; checked 2026-10-04). CDC: "During the 2025-2026 influenza season, the
national baseline is 3.1%", calculated as the mean %ILI during non-influenza weeks of 2022-23,
2023-24 and 2024-25 plus two standard deviations; region-specific baselines use the same method, and
"it is not appropriate to apply the national baseline to regional data." These baselines apply to
the 2025-26 season only.
